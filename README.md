## The six stories

- **Trump, Xi and Putin meet** — the first three-way summit of the US,
  Chinese and Russian leaders, held in Shenzhen on the APEC sidelines;
  90 minutes of talks, no breakthrough.
- **World Court on the Rohingya** — reading its judgment in The Gambia v.
  Myanmar, the ICJ found Myanmar's forces killed about 10,000 Rohingya
  in the 2017 clearance operations.
- **IEA: Hormuz shock** — the World Energy Outlook calls the Strait of
  Hormuz crisis the "largest threat to energy security in history".
- **Bangladesh lockdown** — the Awami League's nationwide lockdown shut
  parts of the country; Muhammad Yunus's family home was set ablaze and
  police said two people were killed.
- **Cyclone Akvan nears** — Odisha, India, evacuated more than 80,000
  people ahead of the storm's expected landfall near Gopalpur.
- **COP31 deal elusive** — the climate talks in Antalya entered their
  final stretch with key texts still unresolved.

## How it works

`entry.png` (1024x1024) is the finished image. `render.py` draws it with
Pillow from flat shapes and the Noto Sans fonts — no photos, no external
assets, no network. `main.py` uses only the standard library and copies
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so every run writes the
same bytes.
